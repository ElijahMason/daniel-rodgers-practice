Sheet 01

Make one that a factory can read.

Daniel Rodgers makes the first physical version of a mechanism or a circuit. He learns from that version. He leaves drawings and files a factory can follow.

What the shop takes on

The work is the first build, and the package a manufacturer can use after it. A print run, a software product, and a finished object for sale are other trades.

  1. 01 Concept models A form you can hold, so a mechanism can still change.
  2. 02 Mechanical prototypes Motion, fit, and load, built until one question is answered.
  3. 03 Electrical prototypes A circuit you can power and probe. The schematic stays honest to the board.
  4. 04 Printed studies A plastic study of a shape, a clip, a hinge, or a hand-fit. He writes the production material separately.
  5. 05 Design-for-manufacture handoff Drawings and files a machinist or a board house can build from.
